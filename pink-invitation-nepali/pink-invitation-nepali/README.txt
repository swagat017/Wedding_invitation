NEPALI PINK WEDDING INVITATION (नेपाली विवाह निमन्त्रणा)
=====================================================
pink-invitation-nepali/
  index.html   Nepali text: names, blessing, bride/groom, form labels
  style.css    colors (top of file), fonts and layout
  script.js    EDIT THE CONFIG AT THE TOP: date (Bikram Sambat), WhatsApp number, phone, email, map link, greeting words
  images/
    background.jpg   hero background (floral backdrop photo)
    couple.png       couple illustration (transparent PNG)
    bride.jpg        bride photo (portrait)
    groom.jpg        groom photo (portrait)

DATE: type the BS year, month number (1 = Baishakh ... 12 = Chaitra), day and weekday in script.js.
      Hearts and the full date are filled in automatically in Nepali numerals. The sample date is only a placeholder.

PREVIEW: double-click index.html (internet needed for fonts).

PERSONAL LINKS: add #Name to the link. Nepali names work too:
  https://yoursite.netlify.app/#राम-थापा  ->  "आदरणीय राम थापा ज्यू,"
  Use hyphens between name parts; letters only.

GO ONLINE (free): app.netlify.com/drop -> drag the whole pink-invitation-nepali folder in.
